About Our Website

The Lost & Found Management System was developed to address the growing challenge of lost and found items within educational institutions and organizations. The idea for this project was initiated by Mustapha Dominick Msopo, with the support and collaboration of his fellow first-year students at Dar es Salaam Institute of Technology (DIT), Tanzania. After observing the increasing number of cases involving misplaced and unclaimed belongings on campus, the team recognized the need for a simple, secure, and efficient digital solution. Their goal was to make it easier for users to report lost items, register found items, upload photos, search for matching records, receive notifications, and help reunite owners with their belongings. This platform promotes honesty, responsibility, and community cooperation by providing a reliable system that improves the management and recovery of lost property.
